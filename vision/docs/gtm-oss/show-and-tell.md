# Discussions — Show & tell

Enable **GitHub Discussions** on the repo. Prefer a category named **Show and tell**.

## What to post

- Thermometer screenshot or readiness % from `curbpack demo` / Action
- Whether you’d recommend it (yes / not yet / no)
- Org or handle **only with consent**

## What not to post

- Claims of certification, CE marking, notified-body approval
- Secrets, customer source, or production credentials
- Auto-scraped LinkedIn outreach dumps

## Weekly automation

`.github/workflows/weekly-discussion.yml` posts a **claim-safe** smoke summary Mondays. Humans still own social amplification (`docs/gtm-oss/` templates).

## Tester cohort

Public checklist toward ~100 activations lives in Discussions + [Tester report](../../.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/tester_report.yml) issues. Private CSVs stay out of this repo.
